# Analytics reference

Anchors read from Salesforce Help on 2026-11-23.

## Surfaces

| Surface | What it gives | Access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Custom report types | Reports on field service objects such as work orders, product requests, maintenance plans and assets | Standard reporting permissions |
| Gantt utilization | Scheduled hours against operating hours per resource and territory. Not calculated for capacity-based resources | Dispatcher console |
| Optimization Insights | Summary, metrics, KPIs by territory before and after a run, and a health check | Managed package |
| Enhanced Scheduling and Optimization dashboard | Automation, success and manual rates, hours saved | Enhanced Scheduling and Optimization |
| Field Service Analytics app | Prebuilt CRM Analytics dashboards for first-time fix rate, utilization, SLA compliance and travel time | Use CRM Analytics Templated Apps, and Access Service Cloud Analytics Templates and Apps |
| Field Service Intelligence | The newer CRM Analytics dashboards, which Help points to as the latest | Requires Data 360, CRM Analytics and Agentforce Service |

## Documented targets

The Enhanced Scheduling and Optimization dashboard page gives these targets: automation rate above 80%, success rate above 98%, manual rate below 20%. A low success rate points to failed optimization requests.
